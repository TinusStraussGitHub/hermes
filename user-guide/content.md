## 📱 For Attendees

### 1. Registration, Login & Forgot Password
- **First Time**: Click "Register", enter your Name, Surname, Company Name, Job Title, Email, and Mobile number.
- **POPIA Consent**: In compliance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you must review the privacy notice and check the consent box to create an account.
- **Returning**: Simply login with your email and password.
- **Forgot Password**: If you forget your password, click the **Forgot your password?** link under the login button. Enter your email and you will receive a secure password reset link directly from Firebase Authentication to easily recover access to your account.
- **Install as App**: On Android (Chrome menu > Install App) or iOS (Safari Share button > Add to Home Screen) to use it like a native mobile app.

### 2. Conference Schedule
- View the full list of sessions for the current year.
- Tap any session to view the **Speaker Bio**, session description, and venue location.
- **Bookmarking (Personal Agenda)**: Tap the bookmark icon on any session to add it to your personal favorites. You can toggle the filter at the top of the schedule to view only your bookmarked sessions, making it easy to build and reference your personalized conference agenda.
- **Rating**: After a session, use the star rating system in the session details to provide feedback.

### 3. Live Updates (Newsfeed)
- The Newsfeed is your home for real-time announcements.
- **Announcements**: Highlighted in Red for urgent conference-wide news.
- **Session Posts**: Highlighted in Blue for specific session updates or interesting facts.

### 4. Live Polling
- When a speaker starts a poll, it will appear instantly in the **Polls** tab.
- Tap your choice to vote. Once you vote, you'll see the real-time percentage results of all attendees.

### 5. Session Q&A
- Select the active session from the dropdown.
- Type your question in the bottom bar and hit send.
- **Upvoting**: See a question you like? Tap the "Thumbs Up" to move it to the top so the speaker sees it first. To prevent spamming, you are limited to exactly **one upvote per question**, and you **cannot upvote your own questions**. Tap the "Thumbs Up" again if you wish to remove your upvote.

### 6. Venue & Directions
- The **Venue** tab shows the CSIR ICC details.
- Tap "Get Directions" to open Google Maps with the precise location already set for navigation.

### 7. BME Connect (Networking Hub)
The **Connect** tab is a digital business card exchange and leads manager built to help you network safely and efficiently.
- **My Card**:
  - Displays your custom attendee badge and a personal, scan-ready **QR Code**.
  - Tap **Edit Card** to update your contact details or toggle **Shared Contact Preferences** (e.g., choose whether to share your email, mobile number, or company/job title). Unsharing a field hides it from other attendees in real-time.
- **Scan QR**:
  - Tap **Start Camera** to activate your device's camera scanner.
  - Scan another attendee's QR code to instantly trade contact details.
  - **Bidirectional Exchange**: To keep networking fast, BME Connect automatically registers a mutual connection. When you scan someone, the system instantly sets up a two-way contact link so both of you see each other under **My Contacts** immediately.
- **My Contacts (Leads Manager)**:
  - Scroll and filter your connections in real-time by search query or lead rating.
  - **Private Lead Rating**: Classify connections privately as **🔥 Hot**, **☀️ Warm**, or **❄️ Cold** to prioritize follow-ups.
  - **Private Follow-up Notes**: Add private summaries, actions, or meeting notes for each contact. These notes are stored securely and are only visible to you.
  - **Save vCard**: Tap **Save vCard** to download a standard `.vcf` contact file directly to your smartphone or computer's address book. The download strictly respects the contact's privacy flags, omitting any hidden fields.
